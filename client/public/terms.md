## 2. SHARIAH COMPLIANCE (AL-MUDARABAH & RAHN)
* **Islamic Principles:** All investment pools, trade allocations, and business activities conducted through this platform operate strictly under Shariah guidelines supervised by our Shariah Board.
* **Contract Structure:** Funds deployed through the platform operate under the Shariah contract of *Mudarabah* (Partnership in Profit), where the Company acts as the *Mudarib* (Manager) or *Rab-ul-Mal* (Capital Provider) matching funds to vetted Halal sector trades (e.g., phone accessories, food commodities, clothing).
* **Prohibition of Riba:** Any transaction involving interest (*Riba*), excessive uncertainty (*Gharar*), or gambling (*Maysir*) is strictly prohibited.
* **Profit & Loss Sharing:** Realized net profits shall be shared at pre-agreed ratios (standard 60% Investor / 40% Manager). In accordance with *Mudarabah* principles, financial losses arising from genuine market conditions are borne by the capital provider. Financial losses resulting from proven negligence, fraud, or breach of trust (*Khianat*) are recovered through our pledged collateral (*Rahn*) frameworks.

* **The 24-Hour Request Window:** Upon broadcast of the profit notification, Members are granted a twenty-four (24) hour operational window to submit a profit withdrawal request to their verified bank account via the platform dashboard.
* **Disbursement:** Approved profit withdrawal requests submitted within the 24-hour window shall be processed and disbursed within 24–48 hours.
* **Automatic Rollover (Re-investment):** Any profit portion not requested for withdrawal within the 24-hour window shall automatically roll over and merge into the Member's active Capital Pool for the subsequent three (3) month market cycle under Shariah rules.
